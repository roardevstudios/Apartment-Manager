# Portfolio dashboard surface

- Mode: Operate
- Product: Private portfolio ledger for one Slovak property owner, with multiple profiles and a local manager workflow for monthly bill entry.
- Character: precise, grounded, quietly navigational.
- World: Central European city wayfinding—its humanist type, route colors, and disciplined station rhythm inform the interface without replacing normal dashboard structure.
- First viewport: standard application sidebar and top header; headline "Prehľad portfólia"; income, costs and net operating result; a revenue/cost timeline; map of property locations; recent property activity.
- Signature: a selected property is traced as a sage route from address to its monthly income and bills; route color appears only in the map and associated figures.
- Risk: sample Slovak property data may be mistaken for real data; label it in the interface and make reset/edit paths obvious.
- Scope: add/edit/archive properties, local profiles, passcode lock, search/filter, monthly utility bill entry with receipt upload, bill history, analytics, Slovak reference source links.
- Responsive: sidebar becomes a compact mobile nav; charts and property list stack; location map remains legible.
